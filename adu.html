01 / A backyard in Utah

Start with the yard.

An ADU starts with the property you already have. This sketch shows one way a small detached home could sit alongside an existing house and deck, with its own entrance and room left for the yard.

For your property, we’d look at where a unit might fit, how people and equipment would get to it, and how water and sewer could reach it. The diagram shows what we’d look at in a free preliminary review before settling on a layout.

02 / A few possibilities

What could it be?

Compact studio

One open room with a kitchenette and separate bath. A starting point when keeping more of the yard matters.

One-bedroom cottage

A bedroom with a door and room to sit with guests. Worth exploring when someone will live there full time.

Garage conversion

Start with a building you already have. We’d check the slab, ceiling height, access, and utility routes before planning around it.

Backyard recording studio

Imagine a rehearsal and recording room local musicians can book by the hour. A separate entry, a small control area, and sound isolation designed for the site would shape the layout.

Quick concept sketches. Each layout would be developed for its site.
