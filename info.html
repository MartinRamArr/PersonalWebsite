Mario Ramirez-Arrazola is a designer from Salt Lake City, Utah. A graduate of Columbia University GSAPP, the University of Oklahoma, and the University of Utah, he freelances and creates domestic objects. He co-founded Marra Roofing (DBA: marra) with his father. His background in economics, the history of technology, and teaching informs a practice spanning architecture, objects, and construction, with an interest in how materials and images shape everyday life.
