Mario Ramirez-Arrazola is a Mexican-American designer from Salt Lake City, now based in Los Angeles. A graduate of Columbia GSAPP, he works at Gensler and co-founded Marra Roofing with his father. His background in economics, the history of technology, and teaching informs a practice spanning architecture, objects, and construction, with an interest in how materials and images shape everyday life. A first-generation graduate, he remains committed to education and immigrant advocacy.
