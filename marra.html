marra (Marra Roofing LLC, doing business as marra and Marra Construction Services) is a family-owned design and construction practice working across the Salt Lake Valley and Boise, Idaho. Founded by a father and son in March 2019, the practice brings design and building together, working with clients from initial ideas and drawings through construction and finishing.

Our story began with our father’s roofing work in Southern California in the early 2000s. Today, his more than 25 years of hands-on experience meet his son’s architectural education, most recently a Master of Architecture from Columbia University GSAPP. marra grows out of that exchange—between generations, between drawing and making, and through a shared commitment to careful, honest work.

We offer residential design, construction, renovation, and repair. Our work includes roofing, siding, soffit and fascia, gutters, windows and doors, decks, patios, fencing, interior remodeling, kitchens and bathrooms, drywall, painting, flooring, finish carpentry, additions, and general repairs. Whether developing a new space or caring for an existing home, we give equal attention to how it looks, how it works, and how it is built.

Licensed for roofing in Utah and general contracting in Idaho, we carry general liability and workers’ compensation insurance. We work directly with homeowners, contractors, and insurance companies, offering free estimates and warranties. Our longstanding relationships within the construction industry also allow us to connect clients with tradespeople, specialists, and suppliers suited to their needs, helping coordinate services beyond our own scope of work.
