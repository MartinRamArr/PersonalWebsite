MARRA

marra (Marra Roofing LLC, doing business as marra and Marra Construction Services) is a family-owned residential design and construction practice serving the Salt Lake Valley and Boise, Idaho. Founded by a father and son in March 2019, it pairs over 25 years of building experience—beginning with the father’s roofing work in Southern California in the early 2000s—with the son’s recent Master of Architecture from Columbia University GSAPP.

We work from initial ideas and drawings through construction, bringing care, detail, and honesty to residential projects. We can design, plan, build, and fabricate at any step of the process. We currently focus more generally on accessory dwelling units (ADUs), residential construction, home additions, roofing, exterior and interior improvements, and whole-home renovations. No job is too small; we cover siding, soffit and fascia, gutters, windows and doors, decks, patios, fencing, additions, kitchen and bathroom renovations, interior finishes, and repairs.

Licensed for roofing in Utah and general contracting in Idaho, we carry general liability and workers’ compensation insurance and offer free estimates and warranties. We work with homeowners, contractors, and insurance companies, drawing on longstanding industry relationships to connect clients with tradespeople, specialists, and suppliers as their projects require.
