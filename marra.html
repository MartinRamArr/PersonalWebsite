What’s on your mind?

A few photos, an address, a problem, or an idea. Tell us what you want to change and what matters most to you.

02 / Work it through

Understand the project.

We discuss the existing conditions, scope, priorities, and budget. For larger ideas, that can mean identifying what needs further design, investigation, or specialist input.

03 / Set a direction

Agree on the next step.

An estimate, a defined scope, or a plan for developing the idea further. The aim is a useful next step, with clear expectations.

A few things
you might ask.

Do you take on smaller projects?

Yes. Repairs and focused improvements are part of the practice, alongside our larger residential ambitions. Send us the details and we’ll discuss whether the project is a good fit.

Can we talk about an ADU or addition?

Absolutely. We’re interested in developing this side of the business. Start by sharing your property and your goals. We can discuss the design work, local requirements, and project team that may be needed.

Where do you work?

Our current focus is the Salt Lake Valley and Boise, Idaho. The work we can undertake depends on the project location, scope, and applicable licensing requirements.

Are you licensed and insured?

Marra holds a Utah roofing license and an Idaho general contractor registration, and carries general liability and workers’ compensation insurance. Contact us to confirm the credentials and coverage applicable to your project.

What should I send to get started?

Your location, a short description, a few photographs, and any timing or budget you already have in mind. Early questions are welcome.
