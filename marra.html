01 / A little about us

From the first conversation to the finishing touches.

A construction company.
With more in mind.

Marra began in roofing. It’s where we learned to pay attention: to materials, to weather, to the details that keep a home working as it should.

Today, we’re building on that foundation. We work on residential repairs, renovations, and improvements, while developing a broader practice around additions, ADUs, and thoughtfully made homes.

We’re a family-owned company with practical experience and new ambitions. We like clear conversations, careful work, and finding the right way forward together.

04 / The office

Family-owned. Forward-looking.

Two generations.
One next chapter.

Marra was founded in 2019 by a father and son. One brings decades of building experience. The other brings a background in architectural design—and a lot of questions about what we could make together.

Our roots are in roofing and hands-on construction. Our ambitions reach further: toward homes, additions, ADUs, and a practice where design and building inform each other.

We’re still growing. What stays constant is our commitment to being involved, working carefully, and treating the people we work with honestly.

06 / A few questions

Before
we begin.

Do you take on smaller projects?
Yes. Repairs and smaller improvements are an important part of what we do. Send us a little information about what you need, and we’ll take it from there.
Can we talk about an ADU or addition?
Absolutely. We’re developing this side of our practice and welcome early conversations. The right approach depends on your property, goals, budget, and local requirements.
Where do you work?
Our work is focused on the Salt Lake Valley and Boise, Idaho. Get in touch with your project’s location and a brief description so we can discuss the fit.
Are you licensed and insured?
We hold a roofing license in Utah and general contractor registration in Idaho, and carry general liability and workers’ compensation insurance. We’ll confirm the applicable credentials and scope for your project.
What should I send you?
A short description, the project location, a few photos, and any timing or budget considerations you already have. Drawings are welcome, but you don’t need them to start a conversation.
