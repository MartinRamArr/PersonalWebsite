info marra morris

marra is a family-owned construction practice working across the Salt Lake Valley and Boise, Idaho. Founded by a father and son in 2019, the practice began in roofing and now brings over 25 years of experience to residential construction, renovation, and repair. Its approach is straightforward: thoughtful design, careful building, and honest work.

Services include roofing, siding, soffit and fascia, gutters, windows and doors, decks, patios, fencing, interior remodeling, kitchens and bathrooms, drywall, painting, flooring, finish carpentry, additions, and general repairs.

Licensed for roofing in Utah and general contracting in Idaho, marra carries general liability and workers’ compensation insurance. The practice works directly with homeowners, contractors, and insurance companies, offering free estimates and warranties.
