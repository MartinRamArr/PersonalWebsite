01 / Finding your direction

What feels like you?

You may know the style you love. You may just know how you want a room to feel. Either is a good place to start.

These are a few examples to get the conversation going. We work across styles and welcome combinations, other references, and ideas that don’t fit a label.

Modern

Open. Precise. Full of light.

You’re drawn to clean lines, generous openings, and rooms that feel connected. We can explore long views, quiet materials, and a clear relationship between indoors and out.

What you might be looking for

A calm retreat, an open gathering space, or a compact home that feels expansive.

How much openness feels right? We study glazing alongside shade, privacy, and the cost of larger openings.

Vernacular

Familiar forms. A sense of place.

You want a home that belongs to its setting. Local building patterns, a familiar roofline, and materials with texture can give a new space a sense of continuity without copying the house next door.

What you might be looking for

An addition that sits comfortably with an older house, or a small dwelling with warmth and character.

What is worth carrying forward? We look at the existing proportions, useful materials, and how the building meets the yard.

Farmhouse

A generous welcome. Room to gather.

You like the ease of a pitched roof, a covered porch, and spaces that draw people together. We can develop that warmth through proportions, natural textures, and the details you touch every day.

What you might be looking for

A sociable kitchen, a shaded place to sit, and a useful transition between the garden and the house.

Which details do the work? We balance the porch, roof, windows, and finishes so the character comes from the whole design.

Somewhere between

Your references don’t have to match.

An old brick house and a crisp new addition. A simple gable with a generous glass opening. You might love several styles, or have an idea that doesn’t fit a label. We help find the relationships that make it feel intentional.

What you might be looking for

Something personal: familiar in some ways, surprising in others, and suited to how you actually live.

What ties it together? A repeated material, a shared proportion, or a carefully placed connection can bring different forms into conversation.

Have something else in mind? Bring it. A photograph, a material, a place you remember, or a way you want to live can be the starting point for your design.

02 / Our design ethos

The look you love.
The life you want.

Style gives us a shared language. We work with you to turn it into a place with its own character: shaped by your routines, your surroundings, and the things you want to make possible.

That means looking closely at the details behind an image you love. Is it the filtered light? The texture of the walls? A generous room opening onto the garden? Understanding the attraction helps us carry it into your project, at a scale and budget that make sense.

How do you want to feel here?

Quiet and tucked away, open and sociable, or a little of both. We translate atmosphere into decisions about light, proportion, materials, and privacy. A beautiful space should also feel comfortable to inhabit on an ordinary Tuesday.

What should this space let you do?

Host a crowded dinner. Work without taking over the kitchen. Keep family close while giving everyone a front door of their own. We build the brief around your particular life, including the uses a standard room label might miss.

Where will your investment matter most?

We compare options for the changes you’ll notice every day. Keeping a useful structure may leave room for a better opening, a sheltered outdoor space, or a material you love. Cost, maintenance, and future changes belong in the design conversation from the beginning.

Residential design

Homes & additions

Custom homes, additions, and backyard homes. We draw around the things that make your project specific, from how you enter the house to where you want the afternoon light.

Renovations & interiors

Rooms in use

Renovation and interior design for rooms that need to work differently. We study layout, light, privacy, finishes, and where a built-in could make better use of the space.

Fabrication

Drawn to be made

Custom pieces and small assemblies, from built-ins to an awning. We use drawings to work out how the parts meet, how they attach, and what a shop or builder needs to make them.

What are you working on?

Send us a few images you love, a photo of your space, and what you’d like to change. If you have a budget or timeline in mind, include that too. You don’t need a finished brief to begin.
